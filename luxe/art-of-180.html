The Art of 180

Some things only time can perfect.

Nearly six months. One composition. No shortcuts.

Every Feminista fragrance is given nearly 180 days to mature, allowing its notes to deepen, soften and come together with greater balance. This patience is not a marketing point — it is the House's only method.
